About

Adrián Rivera Ferrán

Photo and video for consumer brands since 2017, in New York and Puerto Rico.

I'm Adrián Rivera Ferrán, a photo and video creator in New York. For three years I ran the content engine at Chip City Cookies: product, reels, paid video and static ads every month, collabs with Eggo, Pringles and TBS, and the company's training series.

Before that I directed a TV spot for Puerto Rico's largest açaí brand and made content for Nestlé and Magnum at AdWorks. I edit in Premiere and After Effects, and I build AI-assisted workflows and ad creative that keep the product true to life.
