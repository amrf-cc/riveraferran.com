What I collect and why

Contact form. When you send a message, the form collects your name, email address, and message. It is processed by Formspree, which forwards it to my inbox. I use it only to reply to you and to discuss the work you're asking about.

Email. If you email me directly, I receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

I keep correspondence only as long as I need it for the conversation, the project, or my business and tax records.

Services that see your visit

Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Video. Videos on project pages are embedded from Livid. When a project page with video loads, Livid and its delivery network receive your IP address and basic browser details so they can stream the video. See the Livid Privacy Policy.

Fonts. Fonts are served from this site. No requests go to Google Fonts or any other font service.
