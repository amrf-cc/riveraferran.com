Chip City Reel

Cookie Fries — operations

The brief

Chip City sells a rotating weekly menu across its stores, its app, and the delivery platforms. Every weekly drop, new product, and brand collaboration needed photo and video, organic and paid, on a fixed schedule.

What I did

  • Concepted, shot, and edited all photo and video except graphic design, and partnered with the Creative Director to keep every channel on brand.
  • Shot collab launches with Eggo, Pop-Tarts, Pringles, Peeps, Philadelphia Cream Cheese, Recess, Graza, Bee Dolce, Loverboy, SNL 50th Anniversary and TBS.
  • Produced paid video and static ads every month, and made AI-assisted creative that ran live.
  • Made training and internal videos, an HR hiring video, new-location architecture photos, and brand activation coverage.
  • In my final months, built an AI chatbot that lets store staff look up baking procedures in plain language.
