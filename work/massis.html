Massi's Homemade Sandwiches

Opening video and menu photography

Photographer & Videographer · 2025 · New York City

  • Studio product
  • Founder & behind the scenes

Massi's Homemade Sandwiches runs on a simple premise: real food, made from scratch. Semolina sourdough baked in-house, fresh meats sourced with care, everything prepared by hand. When they called me to shoot their soft opening, I was in. A sandwich shop this proud of doing it right — and fully seed oil-free — is rare. After the opening, I shot their entire menu for the delivery apps.
