Episode preview

Sabor y Sazón — Episode 1

Chef Mia Castro is a Puerto Rican chef and cookbook author — Beat Bobby Flay champion, Hell's Kitchen standout, host of Ramsay in 10 — whose debut cookbook, Cocina Puerto Rico, became a bestseller. When Bad Bunny's Super Bowl halftime show put Puerto Rico in the spotlight, she moved fast: a picadera booklet, Puerto Rican snack recipes built for watch parties, exclusive to pre-orders. I shot every image in it. Now I'm behind the camera as cinematographer for Sabor y Sazón, the companion series that turns hand-picked recipes from the book into something you can watch, releasing now.
