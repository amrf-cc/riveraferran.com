Rodowita Water

US launch creative, studio to AI

A Polish mineral water brand's US launch. Real product photography, extended and animated with AI, checked against the real bottle.

Photographer and videographer · 2026–present

  • AI-assisted creative
  • Studio product
To add

Three-step comparison per piece: real studio shot, AI step, final ad

The brief

Rodowita needed launch creative for the US market in a cool, desaturated forest look, across stills and motion, without the product drifting from what's on the shelf.

What I did

  • Shot the product in studio as the reference for everything after.
  • Extended and animated scenes with AI (Nano Banana, Photoshop Generative Extend, Runway, Veo), checking label, color, and shape against the real bottle at every step.
  • Refined every prompt in Wondr Forge, my prompt tool, before generating.

Result

AI-assisted creative that ran live for the launch.
