Gyro King

Menu, store, and operations

Photographer · New York City

  • Studio product
  • Founder & behind the scenes

Gyro King has held the same corner at 50 E. Fordham Road for over 50 years — same owner, same recipes. I shot part of the menu for DoorDash, then stayed for store and operations photos that show how the place works. The brief I gave myself: capture the raw essence of a Bronx institution.
