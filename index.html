About

Food first. Always.

I'm Adrian Rivera Ferran, a photographer and videographer based in New York City, from San Juan, Puerto Rico. I shoot menus, product launches, and social content for restaurants and food brands.

I've been a professional photographer for eight years and in food & beverage since 2019. I've shot for Chip City Cookies, Massi's Homemade Sandwiches, Brasa, Açaí Express, and Sobao by Los Cidrines, plus brand activations for McDonald's and Pepsi. I shoot photo and video end to end: concept, capture, edit, delivery.

Available for freelance food & beverage projects, and open to full-time roles. Let's talk.
